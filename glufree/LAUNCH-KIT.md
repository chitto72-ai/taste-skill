## 2. Post per gruppi Facebook celiaci (IT)

> Ciao a tutti 👋 Da celiaco/a ho sempre faticato a trovare al volo locali
> *davvero* sicuri quando sono fuori o in viaggio. Così ho dato una mano a
> costruire **Glufree**, una mappa gratuita dei posti gluten free con un badge
> chiaro: 100% senza glutine, certificato AIC o menu GF dedicato.
>
> È ancora all'inizio e si arricchisce col passaparola: se conoscete un locale
> da aggiungere, si segnala in un attimo. Mi fa piacere ogni feedback 🙏
> 👉 https://glufree-app.netlify.app/mappa
>
> (P.S. per chi ha celiachia severa: verificate sempre con il locale, la mappa
> è un punto di partenza, non una garanzia medica.)

*Nota: pubblicare solo dove le regole del gruppo lo consentono, e rispondendo a
richieste reali ("dove mangio a…?") più che con post a freddo.*

## 3. Post Reddit (EN) — r/glutenfree, r/Celiac

**Titolo:** I built a free map of dedicated & celiac-safe gluten-free places worldwide

> As a celiac, the hardest part of eating out (especially traveling) is knowing
> whether a place is *actually safe* — dedicated kitchen vs. "we have a GF
> option." So I built **Glufree**, a free interactive map that tags each place
> by safety level: 100% gluten-free, celiac-association certified, or dedicated
> GF menu.
>
> It's early and grows by word of mouth — you can search by city, filter by
> safety, and share a place with one tap. No app store needed (works as a PWA).
> Feedback very welcome, especially on places I should add in your city.
>
> 🔗 https://glufree-app.netlify.app/mappa
>
> Disclaimer: always confirm with the venue if you're highly sensitive — this is
> a starting point, not a medical guarantee.

## 4. Show HN / Product Hunt

**Tagline:** Glufree — the gluten-free map that tells you how *safe* a place is

**Primo commento:**
> Hi! Glufree is a free, install-free (PWA) map of gluten-free restaurants,
> pizzerias and bakeries worldwide. Unlike a generic search, every place is
> tagged by safety level (100% GF kitchen / celiac-certified / dedicated GF
> menu) and restaurant owners can verify their venue. Built with Next.js, no
> mass-scraping of Maps (against their ToS) — a curated, verified DB plus
> optional live Google Places search. Would love feedback from the celiac
> community and makers alike.
